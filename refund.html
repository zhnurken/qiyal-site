Refund Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

We want you to be happy with Qiyal Studio. You can try the core features for free in Lite before buying Pro.

1. 14-day refund

If Qiyal Studio Pro does not work for you, you can request a full refund within 14 days of purchase — for Pro Lifetime and for the first payment of a Pro Subscription.

2. Subscription renewals

You can cancel a subscription at any time; Pro stays active until the end of the paid period. Renewal payments are refundable within 14 days if you have not used Pro features since the renewal.

3. What is not refunded

  • Charges from third-party AI providers (such as kie.ai) for generations made with your own API key — these are paid to the provider, not to us.
  • Requests made after the 14-day period, except where required by law.

4. How to request a refund

Email studioqiyal@gmail.com with your order number, or use the link in your Lemon Squeezy receipt. Refunds are issued by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record, to the original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days. After a refund, the license key is deactivated and the Software returns to Lite.
