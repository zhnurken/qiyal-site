Payments and refunds

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Qiyal Studio is free during the beta, so there is nothing to buy and nothing to refund.

1. Voluntary support

Support of the project (for example, via PayPal or Patreon) is a voluntary donation. It does not unlock features and is processed by that platform under its own rules. If you supported the project by mistake, write to us and we will try to help.

2. AI provider charges

Generations are paid with your own API key directly to the AI provider (such as kie.ai). These charges are paid to the provider, not to us.
